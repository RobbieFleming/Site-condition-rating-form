Section D Assessment:
E Traffic Operations YES = Compliant  |  NO = Non-Conformance
Checklist Item YES NO N/A
Traffic flow is operating safely through and around the worksite
Driver behaviour is appropriate — no evidence of aggressive or unsafe manoeuvres
Traffic delays are within acceptable limits for site and road type
Queue lengths are being managed effectively
No evidence of driver confusion — merge points and lane allocation are clear
Sight distances are adequate at approach, through and exit zones
Section E Assessment:
F Worksite Safety YES = Compliant  |  NO = Non-Conformance
Checklist Item YES NO N/A
Crew have been briefed on TM plan, hazards and emergency procedures
All personnel wearing appropriate PPE — hi-viz, footwear, head protection
Plant and equipment operating safely — exclusion zones maintained
Materials and equipment secured — no risk of entering traffic lanes
Work area housekeeping is acceptable — debris, waste and materials managed
Emergency procedures are understood by site personnel
Section F Assessment:
G Documentation YES = Available & Current  |  NO = Missing or Outdated
Checklist Item YES NO N/A

All required permits are available and current
RCA approvals and consent conditions are available on site
Daily TM records and site logs are completed and up to date
Any changes to TM layout have been documented and approved
Current risk assessment is available and reflects site conditions
